## the shape of the vision and the pr body

this section rides every dispatch; the sprout adds it to the wish.

- the solution is the body. the vision artifact and the pr body lead with what this work builds and why it works
- each ends with a short `## pushback footnote`, placed last: one line per prior decision this work pushes back on
  - the line names the decision, where it was made (a path, a pr, or a brief), and the stat or read that overturns it

- no prior decision pushed back on → no footnote at all. never an empty section, never a "none" line
- a pushback with no stat or read behind it is no pushback: keep the prior decision, or go get the read
