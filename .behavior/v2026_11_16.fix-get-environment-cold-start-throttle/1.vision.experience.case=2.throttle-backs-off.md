# case=2 — a throttle backs off

cells: f05 · f11.

## narrative
another tenant of the account hammers the account api. the sdk's own three attempts all get 429. our retry waits with full jitter, longer each time, and tries again. the third of our attempts succeeds. the caller's test never sees the throttle. a non-throttle error (say, a 500) is not retried by us; it surfaces as today.

  [t1] when the second reply is a throttle

  [t2] when the third reply is ok
