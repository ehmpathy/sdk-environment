# 🌙 dream: with-simple-mutex steals a dead local holder's lock at once

- kind = reseed
- owner = ehmpathy/with-simple-mutex
- caught 2026-11-16

## .the cue that fired
the has-consistent-mechanisms review of sdk-environment's cold-start fix found with-simple-mutex, the paved per-machine lock, and could not reuse it.

## .the gap, precisely
v1 frees a crashed holder's lock only when its lease lapses (readme: "no manual compare-and-set steal ... deferred to v2"). on a local disk, a waiter could know at once that the holder is dead: same host, so `process.kill(pid, 0)` answers. with no such check, a jest worker killed mid-section stalls every later caller for the full lease.

## .the shape of the fix
for local-disk caches, record `{ pid, hostname }` in the lock value. on a held key, a waiter on the same host probes the pid; ESRCH → read-confirm-CAS steal (the v2 steal, scoped to a provably dead local holder). cloud disks keep lease-only.

## .why it is not done in this round
another repo; sdk-environment's fix ships its own pid-aware lock (fulcrum F2). once this lands, sdk-environment can swap to with-simple-mutex in one file.
