## .the cue that fired
sdk-environment#18 review: the commander asked upstream to be taught that fulcrums are indexed `F$N` with `$N` zero-padded to 3 digits, so `F019` always sorts after `F002`.

## .the gap, precisely
- the rule's path shape is `inventory.of=fulcrums.case=F$n-$slug.md`, with `$n` unpadded
- unpadded, a dir sort reads `F1, F10, F11, … F19, F2, F20, F3`: a reader who scans the inventory meets the cases out of order
- the same holds for seeds (`S$n`), which share the shape

## .the shape of the fix
- the rule: `case=F$nnn-$slug`, `$nnn` = the ordinal padded to 3 digits (`F001`, `F019`); the same for `S$nnn` seeds
- any skill or template that mints a fulcrum id pads it
- clamp: mint the 2nd and the 19th fulcrum; assert a plain sort of the dir lists `F002` before `F019`

another repo's rule. this round padded its own 21 fulcrums (`F001`–`F021`).
